Read a date in the Brazilian format (DD/MM/YYYY), display the day, month, and year separately, and determine whether the date belongs to the first or second semester.

Examples:
- 15/03/2026 → Day: 15, Month: 03, Year: 2026. First semester.
- 20/08/2026 → Day: 20, Month: 08, Year: 2026. Second semester.
